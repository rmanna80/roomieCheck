# Roadmap (prioritized)

Ordered by value to real households and risk. Everything here builds on primitives that already
exist (Signal kinds, responses, approvals, notifications).

## v1.1, informed by pilot findings
1. **House Handoffs.** A new Signal kind: "Can someone move my laundry to the dryer?" Accept or decline, never auto-assigned.
2. **Laundry Queue.** Machines as spaces with short windows, plus a voluntary "cycle done" reminder. No fake machine detection.
3. **Household Issues.** Track a broken appliance or internet outage inside the home. Clearly separate it from "Send to landlord" (later integration). Show safety copy: *call emergency services; don't rely on the app*.
4. **Recurring Resets and Sparks.** "Every Sunday 10 min kitchen reset", "Thursday movie night".
5. **Guest & quiet templates.** Friendly wording for overnight guests and parties. A heads-up is never treated as permission.
6. **Report and block** inside a household, before any community layer.

## v1.2
7. **House Constitution.** Proposals, discussion, approval, versioning. Majority votes can't override any individual's privacy or safety.
8. **House Digest.** Optional evening summary, with frequency set per person.
9. **Sign in with Apple and Google.**

## v2
10. **Smart Suggestions.** Transparent rules over opt-in history ("You usually take trash out Sundays — make it recurring?"). No ML profiling, no reading comments.
11. **Optional shared food inventory** with expiry reminders for items people choose to track.
12. **Layer 2: building / residence hall.** Verified, opt-in residency, building events in Explore, lost and found. Needs moderation, reporting and blocking first.
13. **Layer 3: institution / property manager.** Authorized announcements and events only. Never any read access to household data.

## Explicitly not planned
Location tracking, background presence detection, cleanliness rankings, streaks, public blame stats,
ads based on presence or behavior, selling activity data, and a subscription on core coordination.
